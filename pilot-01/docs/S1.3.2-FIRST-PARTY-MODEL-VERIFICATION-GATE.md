# S1.3.2 — First-Party Model Verification Gate

Status: ACTIVE

## Purpose

The frozen `ai-price-index` snapshot is an input for candidate discovery and historical price evidence. It is not authoritative for model identity, launch dates, training cutoffs, or historical snapshot availability.

No row from `registries/s1_3_model_candidates.json` may be promoted into `registries/models.json` solely because it appears in the third-party pricing snapshot.

## Promotion gate

A candidate may enter the canonical Model Registry only when all of the following are true:

1. the provider identity is supported by an official or archived-official first-party source;
2. the provider model name or exact snapshot identifier is supported by that source;
3. any populated `public_launch_date` is supported by `model_identity_evidence.json`;
4. training cutoff remains `unknown` unless separately supported by training-cutoff evidence;
5. historical snapshot availability remains `unknown` unless separately supported under IF-01;
6. pricing references remain point-in-time evidence and do not establish model identity by themselves.

## Candidate quarantine

All extracted candidates begin with:

- `identity_status = CANDIDATE_PENDING_FIRST_PARTY_IDENTITY_EVIDENCE`
- `launch_date_status = UNVERIFIED`
- `training_cutoff_status = UNVERIFIED`
- `historical_snapshot_status = UNVERIFIED`

Unexpected, future-dated, renamed, deprecated, or otherwise unclear candidate names are not corrected by inference. They remain quarantined until first-party evidence resolves them.

## Research-integrity rule

Absence of first-party evidence is represented as unresolved/unknown. DACDM must not invent a launch date, map a rolling alias to a historical snapshot, or substitute a successor model for an unavailable historical model.
