## Research Status

DACDM v1.0 is a conceptual working-paper framework and is not an empirically validated forecasting model. No claim of empirical support is made at this stage.

The accompanying `DACDM_Pilot_01_Protocol_v1.0` is frozen as of 2026-08-14 and defines the first pre-registered empirical attempt to subject H2–H4 to falsification in the code-generation domain.
